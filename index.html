Sift

AI spam filtering for iCloud Mail, running on your own hardware.

Free and open source. Requires macOS 14 or later.

Why Sift.

Catches what iCloud misses
An AI classifier reads each new message the moment it arrives and moves spam to Junk at the confidence you choose.
Runs on your hardware
The filter runs around the clock on a Mac, Linux box, or Raspberry Pi. Sift installs it over SSH, and your iCloud password never leaves that machine.
Clears the backlog
Scan years of old mail, review what Sift found, and clean it up in one pass.
Reports the senders
Sift sends abuse reports to the sender's network, registrar, link hosts, and phishing clearinghouses, with your address removed from the evidence.
Unsubscribes for you
Sift lists the newsletters you keep getting and leaves the ones you don't read in one click.
Keeps real mail safe
Trusted senders skip the filter. Brand senders only count as trusted when they pass DMARC, so phishing that fakes them still gets caught.

Set up in minutes.

  1. 1

    Open Sift and pick the computer that will run your filter.

  2. 2

    Add an iCloud app-specific password and a TypeSafe or OpenRouter API key.

  3. 3

    Choose which kinds of spam to block. Sift handles the rest.

Sift has no account and no subscription. Messages are classified by the AI provider you pick, billed to your own API key.
