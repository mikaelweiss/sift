Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Overview

Sift is a Mac app and a server you run on your own computer to filter spam from iCloud Mail. Sift is published by Weiss Solutions LLC ("we", "us").

We collect nothing. Sift has no account, no analytics, and no servers of ours. Your mail and settings stay on hardware you control, and leave it only for the services described below.

What Stays on Your Hardware

The Sift server stores, on the computer you install it on:

  • Your iCloud email address, and your app-specific password (encrypted)
  • Your AI provider API key (encrypted)
  • Your settings, categories, and allowlist
  • Classification results, scan history, and abuse report history
  • The Macs you have paired with it

The Mac app stores its connection details and pairing token in the macOS Keychain.

What Leaves Your Hardware

Sift sends data only where its features need it:

  • Apple iCloud Mail. The server reads your mail and moves spam to Junk over IMAP. Abuse reports go out from your iCloud address over SMTP. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Your AI provider. For each new message, the server sends the sender, reply-to address, subject, authentication results, linked domains, and the first 2,500 characters of the body to the provider you choose (TypeSafe or OpenRouter), using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies.
  • Abuse reports, if you turn them on. Reports go to the sender's network, registrar, email service, link hosts, and phishing clearinghouses such as APWG and Netcraft. Your address is removed from the evidence. Finding those contacts means looking up the sender's domains and IP addresses in public RDAP and WHOIS directories.
  • Unsubscribe, when you choose it. Sift sends the sender's one-click unsubscribe request.
  • Updates. The app checks sift.weisssolutions.org for new versions and downloads them from GitHub. The check shares your app and macOS versions.

Deleting Your Data

All of it is yours. Delete the server's data folder (~/.local/share/sift) and the Sift app to remove everything. We hold no copy.
